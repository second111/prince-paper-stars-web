## Unapproved audition candidates

The files in `assets/audio/candidates/` are from the CC0
[100 CC0 SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx) pack by rubberduck.
They are not connected to gameplay until the selected versions are approved.
The approved `assets/audio/sfx/memory_reward.wav` is generated in-house by
`tools/generate_ui_sfx_candidates.mjs`; it does not use third-party samples.
