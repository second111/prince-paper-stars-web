# 玫瑰星球 · 3D 资产提示词

本文件沿用 `建模参考图提示词.md` 与 `星球底模生成提示词.md` 的既有体块语言。每张图只用于一个独立资产，先作为建模参考图，再导入图生 3D 工具。

## 统一风格

每段提示词都保持：

- 暖白纯色背景，完整物体居中。
- 正交或轻微透视的三分之四正视镜头。
- 均匀柔光，不投射地面阴影。
- 儿童绘本式低多边形纸雕和刻木玩具。
- 概括、厚实的体块，干净平面切面，不做写实微纹理。
- 减少细小零件，以整体轮廓和主要结构取胜。

## 1. 星球基底

目标是生成一整颗完整球体，供房屋、玫瑰、火山和装饰物独立摆放。基底不能是光滑空球，但中央仍要保留清楚的摆放空间。

```text
One single game-ready 3D asset of the B-612 rose-planet base, presented as a
three-view orthographic turnaround on a plain warm-white background. The image
contains exactly three equal-sized orthographic panels of the SAME planet:
left = top view looking straight down along the polar axis,
center = front view looking horizontally at the equator,
right = side view rotated exactly 90 degrees.
Not three different planets. Three projections of one identical object.

Premium handcrafted papercraft and carved-clay art-toy style for a storybook
low-poly 3D diorama. Softly rounded geometric facets, clean flat-shaded color
planes, only three or four tonal steps per material, matte paper and clay
surfaces, subtle worn edges, no photographic texture and no high-frequency noise.

Absolute shape rule: one complete continuous sphere. No horizontal floor plane,
no terrain slab, no platform, no pedestal and no stand. In the front and side
views, the entire lower hemisphere must curve inward and remain visible.
Do not cut the bottom flat. Do not make an egg, teardrop or floating island.

Upper terrain: a shallow spherical moss cap with irregular, organically stepped
terraces that follow the planet's curvature. Add a few chunky angular rock
ledges, shallow dips and layered paper folds. The terrain must feel full and
inhabited without buildings, but must not become dense clutter.

Surface detail: cover the upper hemisphere with broad moss-green and muted
emerald patches. Add short grass tufts, small muted-olive shrubs, tiny cream and
dusty-pink flower clusters and several pale-gray folded-paper stones. Place a
compact group of three or four simple round-canopy trees near the rear edge.
Keep small ground detail distributed across the upper surface rather than
leaving large bare smooth areas.

Placement zones: leave one subtle shallow rose terrace near the upper center,
one small house clearing on the left and two compact volcano footings on the
right. These are only gentle flattened spherical-cap areas, not platforms.
Preserve clear footprints for later editable props. Do not include the rose,
glass dome, cottage, volcanoes, fence, gold ring or character.

Orthographic setup: three identical views arranged left-to-right with narrow
equal gutters, equal scale and 15 percent empty margin around every sphere.
Soft frontal ambient lighting with restrained form shading, no ground shadow.

Game-ready stylized model, approximately 70k-100k triangles,
one shared moss material and one pale-rock material,
no text, no labels, no ring and no separately modeled cloud.
```

## 生成顺序

1. 先做星球基底三视图，确认完整球体、台地和四个摆放区域。
2. 用基底直径 `D=100` 校准玫瑰、玻璃罩、小屋和火山。
3. 再做路径、树林、栅栏与小摆件。
4. 最后生成小王子与金色环。
5. 所有参考图通过后，再分别导入图生 3D；不要把完整场景一次性生成。
