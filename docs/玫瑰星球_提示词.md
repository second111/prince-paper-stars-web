# 玫瑰星球 · 提示词

## 1. 星球整体主视觉

参考基准：

- 以国王星球主视觉图为准，统一材质精度、电影感光线、镜头高度和主体与星球的尺度。
- 以现有 B-612 玫瑰星球效果图为准确认布局、轮廓和配色。
- 不使用简化低多边形、平面大色块、空球体或写实摄影风格。

### 英文主提示词

```text
Create one polished 1:1 key visual of the complete B-612 rose planet for a high-end
stylized 3D narrative game. Use the supplied King planet main visual as the primary
reference for material richness, cinematic lighting, camera elevation, level of detail,
and the visual relationship between the planet and its top settlement. Use the supplied
rose planet reference only to preserve the identity and placement of the rose, glass
dome, cottage, volcanoes, path and gold ring. Do not copy the King's castle.

This must feel like the same art series as the King main visual: a sophisticated
handcrafted paper-clay miniature with detailed sculpted surfaces, soft organic edges,
subtle paper fiber, gentle painted texture and high-detail game-concept rendering.
Do not simplify it into flat low-poly geometry, broad empty color planes, plastic toy
shapes, clean mathematical spheres or a mobile-game diorama.

Composition:
One complete round moss-covered planet, centered and fully visible in a square frame,
seen from a slightly elevated three-quarter camera. The planet occupies about 72–78% of
the total visual height. Its lower hemisphere is fully rounded and sculpted, never cut
off, pointed or resting on a stand.

Scale hierarchy:
The settlement is substantial and wraps across the upper hemisphere, not a tiny cluster
perched on a flat cap. The buildings and landmark trees together span about 65–80% of
the planet's visible width and occupy roughly the upper 35–45% of the globe. The whole
planet remains the dominant mass.

The rose and glass dome are the tallest focal point, with the dome about 30–35% of the
planet diameter wide. The cottage is about 22–27% of the planet diameter wide and 20–25%
high. Each volcano is about 15–20% of the planet diameter. Large trees are about 15–20%
of the planet diameter high. These proportions should closely match the supplied rose
planet reference, not the tiny structures in a simplified toy version.

Environment and objects:
The moss terrain is densely sculpted with layered organic slopes, curved terraces,
small ridges, irregular moss patches and a few exposed faceted stones. The winding
stone path is wide enough to read clearly, crosses the upper hemisphere and visually
connects the front edge, cottage and rose platform.

At the top center grows one large romantic crimson rose under a thin, clear glass dome.
The rose has richly layered petals, a sculpted stem and a small cluster of leaves, but
remains a stylized game asset rather than a photographic flower. The dome has crisp,
thin glass edges with subtle reflections and does not obscure the rose.

On the left stands a small cream-white cottage with a warm terracotta roof, a slender
chimney and glowing windows. On the right are two rugged miniature volcanoes, one with
a restrained ember glow and a curled ribbon of pale smoke. Add a compact wooden fence,
young baobab sprouts, rounded trees, low flower clusters, mossy bushes and scattered
stones with the same editorial density as the supplied rose reference.

A delicate antique-gold orbital ring circles the planet near the equator, with two small
spheres and one four-point star detail. Keep the ring thin and luminous without letting
it dominate the scene.

Lighting and background:
Deep indigo-violet night sky with tiny warm and cool stars. Long, soft paper-cut mist
ribbons drift behind the planet at several depths. Warm golden key light from the upper
left, cool violet-blue rim light from the lower right, and a faint warm glow from the
cottage and active volcano.
Rich cinematic lighting with soft shadows, controlled highlights and gentle atmospheric
depth. The final image should match the premium illustrated-3D finish of the King main
visual, with the rose planet clearly belonging to the same universe.

No characters, text, UI, logos or frame. Only one complete planet in the image.
```

### 中文执行说明

```text
只生成一颗完整的玫瑰星球主视觉，不生成双态资产图，不生成棋盘格背景。

风格必须接近国王星球主视觉：高细节、柔和写实、电影级光影、纸雕与黏土微缩模型，
允许表面有细腻纸纹、颜料层、石头切面、苔藓起伏和真实的柔和阴影。
不要做成扁平的简洁低多边形，不要把星球做成一颗空球，也不要把顶部建筑缩成小摆件。

星球本体约占总高度 72%–78%；顶部聚落要铺开在星球上半球，总宽度约为星球直径的
65%–80%，垂向占整颗星球高度的上 35%–45%。
玻璃罩宽度约为星球直径的 30%–35%；小屋宽度约 22%–27%；每座火山约 15%–20%；
大树高度约 15%–20%。这些比例以现有玫瑰星球参考图为准。

星球下半球必须完整圆润、有真实隆起和凹凸，不可截底。顶部是连续苔藓坡地，不能像
在一颗光滑球体上放一块平板。路径、木栅栏、树、幼年猴面包树、小花、石块应分布在上
半球并和地形自然结合。

玫瑰位于顶部中央，玻璃罩清晰通透。小屋在左侧，火山在右侧，金色轨道环绕球体。
背景使用深靛紫星空和数条长而柔和的纸片云带，主光从左上照入，右下有冷紫边缘光，
小屋窗户和火山口提供少量暖光。
```

### 图片参考顺序

1. 国王主视觉图：决定材质完成度、细节密度、光影、镜头和整体电影感。
2. 玫瑰星球参考图：决定 B-612 的轮廓、物件摆位、主色和建筑尺度。
3. 本提示词中的比例约束：防止生成“大空球 + 小摆件”的错误构图。

### 生成后检查

- 若看起来像扁平低多边形，强化：`ultra-detailed stylized 3D, sculpted paper-clay miniature, cinematic product-quality game key art, rich surface detail`。
- 若顶部物件太小，强化：`settlement spans 65–80% of planet diameter, large rose dome, larger cottage, integrated upper-hemisphere terraces`。
- 若星球过空，强化：`dense sculpted moss terrain, winding path covering the upper hemisphere, small ridges and organic ground variation`。
- 若过于写实，加入：`stylized handcrafted paper-clay materials, painted game concept art, soft illustrated edges`。
